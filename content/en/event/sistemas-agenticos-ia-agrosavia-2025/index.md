It defines agents and presents ecosystems (LangChain/LangGraph, n8n, Google ADK, Agno, Unsloth), protocols (MCP, A2A, AG-UI), context engineering and coding assistants (Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Windsurf, Cursor, NotebookLM).
